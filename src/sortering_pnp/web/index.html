    What the camera sees and how

    Camera

    Calibration

    1. Print the board at 100 % and glue it flat on card:
      Measured square
    2. Printer homed, tray empty, lights as when sorting.

    Skip for now

    Without calibration: straight down from this height, an ideal lens.

    How it works
    1. Park: the bed goes to the photo position.
    2. Lock (about a minute): exposure, gain, white balance and focus are measured on the centre of the picture (the tray, not a white bin at the side) and frozen.
    3. Lens: move the board slowly over the whole picture; photos are taken by themselves, covered parts turn green.
    4. Position: lay the board flat in the tray and let go.
    5. Then redo Camera to printer (Setup, step 6).

    Camera cropping

    The model sees only the tray's inside; the rest is painted over (same picture size). Needs the layout and Camera to printer.

    edge0mm colour

    + keeps more wall, − cuts more floor.

    Bins view

    When it looks into the bins (start-up check, Resync, after a bin change), the model sees only the floors of the bins whose codes are in the picture; their walls and codes are painted over. Each floor is found from its code, so it follows the bins wherever they stand.

    edge0mm

    + keeps more of each floor's edge, − paints more of it. Snippets: each bin's floor cut out, as the model sees it: check every bin is there, whole.

    Print bins

    Bins, trays, baseplates and holders, ready to print

    Bin sizes

    The code tells the camera a bin's size, and whether it sits on a back shelf or a corner post (codes 50–99; best for the tray). One build plate per size: change colour at the rim (21 mm for 3u, 42 mm for 6u) for black codes.
