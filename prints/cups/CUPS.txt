Suction cups for the suction picker (sortering-pnp print catalogue: prints/cups/)

Material: TPU 95A. 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls (or more: the folds are walls only),
100 % infill, 20-25 mm/s, no supports. Stand it on the shank's end (as exported), tip up.
The folds and the lip are one line of a 0.4 nozzle (they must give), the cup's wall 0.6 mm,
every hole at least Ø1.2: checked by slicing every cup (Bambu Studio, classic and Arachne
walls). Slow down for the folds (15-20 mm/s) if the slicer allows.

Fit: the Ø7 shank goes 7 mm into the picker's socket; 3 M3 screws, 120 deg apart, 3.5 mm from
the socket's bottom, press into its blind holes (Ø2.6, 1 mm deep: they don't reach
the bore, nothing leaks). Snug them, don't crush the TPU.

Length: every cup, normal or wide, is 37 mm from the shank's end to the lip (the neck
makes up the difference, 10 mm or more below the socket, 4 mm or more with the
wide bellows): swap cups without measuring the tip height again.

Body: straight Ø7, bore Ø4.3 (the pump's tube; Ø3.2 in the shank,
behind the screws). The normal set's folds go inwards: no channel is wider than 4.3 mm.

Wide set (_wide): the same tips and holes on a wider bellows, Ø9.5 outside, folds
1.6 mm deep every 3.2 mm (the normal ones Ø5.2, 0.85 mm every 2.4 mm), out from the
neck and back in at 45 deg, the same one-line walls. The bellows is a chamber, not a channel:
inside it is Ø5.4 at its narrowest (wider than the tube); the shank, the neck and the holes are
the same. About 3.5 times softer along the axis (a fold's rate goes with diameter x wall^3 /
depth^3: the deeper folds win over the bigger diameter), 1.7 times softer sideways, as stiff
to tilt. On a sealed part the vacuum pulls it shut (about 2.6 N at -60 kPa, against 0.7 N on
the normal bellows): it lifts the part by a few mm and holds it on its closed folds.

Tips (each hole smaller than the smallest head the tip picks: the head lies on the lip and
closes the cup, nothing goes in):
  AS       lip Ø4.4, 3 folds, Ø5.2 bellows, hole Ø2.2 (no sieve), neck 18.2 mm: M2-M3 heads, flat faces
  AM       lip Ø6.2, 3 folds, Ø5.2 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 3 x Ø1.5), neck 17.2 mm: M4-M5 heads, flat faces
  DS       lip Ø5.2, 4 folds, Ø5.2 bellows, hole Ø2.2 (no sieve), neck 14.8 mm: M2-M3, curved or tilted
  DM       lip Ø7.6, 4 folds, Ø5.2 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 4 x Ø1.5), neck 13.3 mm: M3-M5, curved or tilted
  DL       lip Ø10.6, 4 folds, Ø5.2 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 8 x Ø1.5), neck 11.2 mm: M5-M6 (M8), curved
  AS_wide  lip Ø4.4, 3 folds, Ø9.5 bellows, hole Ø2.2 (no sieve), neck 12.4 mm: M2-M3 heads, flat faces
  AM_wide  lip Ø6.2, 3 folds, Ø9.5 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 3 x Ø1.5), neck 11.4 mm: M4-M5 heads, flat faces
  DS_wide  lip Ø5.2, 4 folds, Ø9.5 bellows, hole Ø2.2 (no sieve), neck 8.2 mm: M2-M3, curved or tilted
  DM_wide  lip Ø7.6, 4 folds, Ø9.5 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 4 x Ø1.5), neck 6.7 mm: M3-M5, curved or tilted
  DL_wide  lip Ø10.6, 4 folds, Ø9.5 bellows, hole Ø2.5 (sieve: 8 x Ø1.5), neck 4.6 mm: M5-M6 (M8), curved
A: three folds and a cone lip, for flat faces (pan, button, cheese and hex heads, nuts).
D: four folds and a skirt flaring to a one-line edge: a few mm of give, follows curved and
   tilted faces (the side of a head, countersunk heads).
_sieve (AM, DM, DL): a dome with a ring of holes instead of one. The part rests on the dome's
   flat top, 0.4 mm below the lip, so the holes on its sides stay open: it can't plug the cup,
   more air flows, and it lets go at once. Holding force is the same (vacuum x the lip's area).
   AS and DS have none: a 0.4 nozzle can't print one in their Ø3 cup (and on AS the head rests
   on the lip, well above the hole).
